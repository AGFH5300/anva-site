How ANVA supports vessel operations

Based at Dubai Maritime City, ANVA Marine coordinates planned and urgent vessel requirements. Its scope includes sourcing machinery spares from maker references, supporting vessel repair attendance and providing technical follow-up through drydocking and maintenance projects. The location and timing of each attendance or supply are confirmed against the vessel's requirements.

For a faster technical response, send the vessel name, location/port, ETA, maker and model, serial or part number, technical photographs, drawings where available and your required delivery or attendance date.

What purchasers and technical teams should send

  • Marine spare parts: vessel or equipment, maker, model, serial number, item description, part reference, quantity, urgency and delivery destination.
  • Riding squad attendance: port, ETA/ETD, work list, shipboard constraints, material status and preferred attendance dates.
  • Drydocking or repair projects: vessel particulars, tentative docking schedule, repair specifications, drawings and the areas in which owner-side support is needed.
  • Specialist products: system application, existing equipment details, required certificates and requested specification.

Common questions

Does ANVA Marine supply marine spares in Dubai?

ANVA Marine handles technical and commercial spares enquiries from its Dubai base. Specific availability, delivery times and maker routes depend on the part reference and destination.

Can ANVA arrange a riding squad for a vessel in port?

Yes, riding squad and onboard repair support can be evaluated based on vessel location, attendance window, work scope and local access requirements.

Does ANVA assist owners during drydocking?

ANVA provides drydocking planning and technical follow-up support. The extent of owner-side representation or site attendance is agreed for each project.

How do I request a marine equipment quote?

Use the ANVA contact page or send the vessel, maker, model, part details and delivery requirements to info@anva-group.com.
