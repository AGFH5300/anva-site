Frequently asked questions

Can I order welding cable from Dubai?

ANVA Marine supports UAE welding cable requests. The chosen size, quantity and delivery date are confirmed at quotation.

Is H01N2-D the only suitable welding cable type?

No. It is one recognised flexible welding cable designation; selection depends on equipment and application requirements.
