Privacy Notice

How Advanced Nautical Ventures FZE handles business enquiries and limited website analytics.

Updated 9 November 2026

1. Who operates this website?

Advanced Nautical Ventures FZE (ANVA Marine), Dubai Maritime City, F1B-W228, Dubai, United Arab Emirates. Contact: info@anva-group.com.

2. Business enquiries and contact details

When you submit an RFQ or service enquiry, we collect the name, company, work email, optional phone, enquiry category, technical request and any product interest you enter. We also record the date, enquiry source page and your agreement to be contacted. We use this information only to evaluate the request, provide a quotation and follow up on the potential business relationship.

The form is processed by a secure backend hosted on Supabase. Enquiry records are held in a restricted database with public access disabled. Technical and organisational measures are used to reduce spam and prevent unauthorized access.

3. Visitor statistics

We measure website activity to understand which pages generate relevant business enquiries. The visitor statistics service may record the date/time of a page view, URL path, referring website domain, marketing source, approximate country when technically available, and broad browser and device type. Our purpose-built tracker does not set advertising cookies, build advertising profiles, use cross-site identifiers, store raw IP addresses or track keystrokes or form entries. Automated bots may be excluded from counts. These are estimates of page visits, not verified identities of individual visitors.

4. Data retention and sharing

Business enquiry records are retained for necessary customer service and commercial follow-up, subject to applicable recordkeeping requirements and deletion requests. Website measurement events are automatically deleted after 90 days. Any anti-spam IP-derived hash linked to an enquiry is cleared after seven days; the original IP address is not saved in these records. We do not sell visitor or enquiry data.

5. International services

The website and enquiry database may be processed by approved service providers outside the UAE. We apply appropriate access restrictions and review transfers against applicable legal requirements. Contact us if you need more information about a specific enquiry.

6. Your choices

You can request access, correction or deletion of the contact information you submitted, or object to further marketing contacts, by emailing info@anva-group.com. We may need to verify your request before changing records, and may retain records where required by law.

7. Questions

Email info@anva-group.com with privacy questions, including inquiries about our use of website analytics. For technical quotation enquiries see our contact form.
